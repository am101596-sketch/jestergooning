WHAT THE PROBABILITY MEANS

A prediction inside a synthetic world.

The target is an on-time first attempt, conditional on a feasible plan and your risk assumptions. Training excludes declined plans. Different random seeds separate training and test cases, but both use the same generator.

The model does not use Census records as outcome labels. It does not determine eligibility, book services, or certify a real job start.

Simulator assumptions: fixed offers, no competition for capacity, independent withdrawals and funding failures, and exponential transport delays. Changing the model of the world may change the results.

Applicant details, provider offers, bridge budgets, transport schedules, outcomes, and stress scenarios are demo fixtures. They are separate from the research directory.

Live use needs verified partners, consent, current capacity, booking authority, privacy controls, and an evaluated service model.

What the research does—and doesn’t—establish

Childcare is a documented employment barrier. These data do not establish how often circular commitments prevent job starts, prove this product’s impact, or determine eligibility. Some local childcare programs support parents who are seeking work.
